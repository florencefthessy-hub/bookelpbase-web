    How It Works

    An author interview happens online, by message, at your pace. No travel, no studio and no stress.

    1

    Say hello

    Send your book title, genre and a link to where it is sold, by email or WhatsApp.

    2

    Get your questions

    You receive a short set of questions about your book, your writing and your readers.

    3

    Answer online

    Reply in your own words, whenever suits you. Short or long, it is your story.

    4

    Go live

    Your spotlight page appears with your cover, your answers and a link for readers to buy.

    Start my interview

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I get my book featured?

    Send a message by email or WhatsApp with your name, your book title, its genre and a link to where it is sold. You will hear back with the next steps.

    How does an online interview work?

    Questions are sent to you by message. You answer them in your own words, and your answers appear on your spotlight page.

    Which genres do you feature?

    A mix: fantasy, historical fiction, mystery and thrillers, romance, magical realism and feel-good fiction. If your book tells a good story, get in touch.

    Where can readers buy a featured book?

    Every spotlight page has a Buy on Amazon button under the interview, so readers can go straight to the book.

    Can you guarantee sales?

    No one can. A feature helps readers discover your book, but sales and rankings depend on many things outside anyone’s control.

    Have a book readers should meet?

    Tell us about it, and let’s give your story its own spotlight page.

    About Bookelpbase

    Bookelpbase is an independent literary discovery platform created to support authors and make discovering remarkable books easier for readers.

    We understand that writing a book takes creativity, dedication and commitment. However, getting that book noticed by the right audience can be another challenge.

    That is where Bookelpbase comes in.

    Our platform provides opportunities for authors to introduce their books through thoughtfully presented features, author interviews and book spotlights.

    We aim to create a welcoming space where writers can share their journeys, readers can discover interesting titles, and great stories can receive more attention.

    Our goal is simple: to help bring authors and readers closer, one story at a time.
